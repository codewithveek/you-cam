## 1. Company basics: founding, HQ, listing, headcount, offices, and a pending take-private deal

### Takeaway
Perfect Corp. was spun out of CyberLink in 2015 and is led by founder, Chairwoman and CEO Alice H. Chang. It is headquartered in New Taipei City, Taiwan, and had 353 employees at the end of 2025. It listed on the NYSE in October 2022 through a merger with the SPAC Provident Acquisition Corp. **The most important current fact: on 2026-07-10 the company signed a definitive agreement to be taken private by an entity Alice Chang controls, at US$2.00 per share. The shareholder vote is on 2026-10-19 and closing is expected in Q4 2026.** The hackathon sponsor is therefore about to become a privately held company.

### Cited Findings
- CyberLink announced the launch of Perfect Corp. as a spin-off on April 15, 2015, to focus on mobile beauty apps (YouCam Makeup, YouCam Perfect). — [CyberLink press room](https://www.cyberlink.com/eng/press_room/view_3825.html) (date taken from the search-result summary)
- The company describes itself as "Founded in 2015… a leading AI company offering self-developed AI- and AR-powered solutions." Its B2C side runs the "family of YouCam consumer apps and web-editing services." Its B2B side "empowers major beauty, skincare, fashion, jewelry, and watch brands and retailers… through AR product try-ons and AI-powered skin diagnostics." — [Q2 2026 results 6-K, 2026-07-27](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1899830/000189983026000029/a2026q2financialresults_pe.htm)
- SPAC route: the de-SPAC partner was Provident Acquisition Corp., which "ceased to exist on October 28, 2022" when the business combination closed. Provident's own IPO was on January 12, 2021. — [FY2025 Form 20-F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1899830/000189983026000013/perf-20251231.htm)
- HQ is at 14F, No. 98 Minquan Road, Xindian District, New Taipei City, Taiwan. The core R&D team is in Taiwan. The company has offices in **Taiwan, Japan, the United States, France and China**, and all offices are leased. — [FY2025 20-F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1899830/000189983026000013/perf-20251231.htm)
- Press contacts listed in July 2026 cover Corporate/Taiwan, USA, Japan, China, Europe, UAE and India. — [WeAreDevelopers press release via AOL/Business Wire, 2026-07-08](https://www.aol.com/articles/perfect-corp-showcases-ai-skin-110000000.html)
- **Employees:** 321 (end 2023), 342 (end 2024), **353 (end 2025)**. By function at end 2025: R&D 180 (51.0%), Sales & Marketing 149 (42.2%), G&A 24 (6.8%). 86% of R&D staff hold a master's degree. "90% of the senior management team" has been with the company since inception. — [FY2025 20-F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1899830/000189983026000013/perf-20251231.htm)
- Patents: 43 registered and 22 pending in the beauty-tech domain as of 2025-12-31. — [FY2025 20-F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1899830/000189983026000013/perf-20251231.htm)
- **Take-private timeline:**
  - 2026-03-18: the board received a non-binding proposal from CyberLink and Alice H. Chang (with her controlled entities) at **US$1.95/share** in cash. — [6-K 2026-03-18](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1899830/000189983026000015/a20260318_perfectcorp-6kxe.htm)
  - 2026-03-23: a special committee was formed (Philip Tsao as chair, Christine Jih, Frank Lee). — [6-K 2026-03-23](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1899830/000189983026000018/a20260323_perfectcorp-6kxe.htm)
  - 2026-04-20: Kroll was appointed financial advisor and DLA Piper legal counsel. — [6-K 2026-04-20](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1899830/000189983026000021/a20260420_perfectcorp-6kxe.htm)
  - 2026-07-10: definitive merger agreement with **ProjectNY** (controlled by Alice H. Chang) at **US$2.00/share**. That is a 48.1% premium to the 2026-03-17 close and 39.6% to the 30-day VWAP. The deal is funded from company cash. The Chairwoman Parties and CyberLink hold about **53.4% of shares and 81.2% of voting power** and have signed support agreements. Closing is expected in Q4 2026, after which the shares would be delisted from the NYSE. — [6-K Ex. 99.1, 2026-07-10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1899830/000110465926082398/tm2619936d1_ex99-1.htm)
  - The extraordinary general meeting (EGM) to vote on the merger is called for **2026-10-19** in New Taipei City, with a record date of 2026-09-08. — [6-K Ex. 99.1, 2026-08-26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1899830/000110465926101026/tm2624065d1_ex99-1.htm)

### Inferences
- Insiders hold 81.2% of voting power and the deal needs a two-thirds vote of shares present. Approval at the 2026-10-19 EGM therefore looks very likely, so the company will probably be private during or soon after the hackathon's judging (deadline 2026-11-02). After that, public financial disclosure (6-Ks and 20-Fs) will probably stop.
- Going private suggests management wants room to restructure toward API and agentic AI products without quarterly public scrutiny, given flat revenue and a shrinking enterprise base (see Section 2). This is my interpretation. The filings do not state a rationale beyond the merger terms.

### Gaps
- The exact headcount for 2026 is not disclosed. The 20-F gives 2025 year-end only.
- I could not confirm the precise spin-out legal date. Wikipedia lists a June 1, 2015 founding date ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perfect_Corp)), while CyberLink's announcement is dated April 15, 2015.

## 2. Financials: revenue 2023–2026, mix, growth, profitability, subscribers, guidance

### Takeaway
- **Annual revenue:** $53.5M (2023), $60.2M (2024, +12.5%), **$69.2M (2025, +14.9%)**.
- **2026 so far:** Q1 revenue $17.9M (+12.0%), Q2 $16.3M (flat), H1 $34.3M (+5.9%).
- **What drives growth:** almost all of it is the **B2C YouCam app and web subscription business**, which earned $47.0M in 2025, about 68% of revenue. The B2B enterprise segment is "cautious," and Key Customers fell from 139 to 113 year over year.
- **Profitability:** gross margin rose above 80% as the company moved from custom software to "standardized AI/API solutions," and it reached IFRS operating profit for H1 2026.
- **Subscribers:** paying subscribers peaked at about 1.0M (end 2024) and fell to 820K by mid-2026.
- **Guidance:** the FY2026 guide of about +10% (±2%), given in Feb 2026, was not repeated in either the Q1 or Q2 2026 release.
- **API revenue:** not disclosed separately.

### Inferences
- 2024 growth works out to about 12.5% ($60.2M / $53.5M).
- Implied non-B2C revenue in 2025 is about $22.2M ($69.2M − $47.0M), roughly 32% of the total. That covers enterprise SaaS, licensing and any other items. I derived this figure; the company does not report it.
- B2C grew at a 30% CAGR from 2023 to 2025, which means B2B and enterprise revenue was roughly flat to down over the period ($53.5M − $27.7M = $25.8M in 2023 vs. about $22.2M in 2025). This lines up with the falling Key Customer count.
- To reach even the low end of the FY2026 guide ($76.1M), H2 2026 revenue must be about $41.8M. H2 2025 was about $36.8M ($69.2M − $32.4M), so H2 would need roughly +13.6% growth, after a flat Q2. The guide looks at risk, and the company has stopped repeating it.
- The strategic story underneath the numbers: B2C subscribers are shrinking under GenAI app competition, enterprise brand spend is contracting, and margins are improving by standardizing on APIs. **The self-serve API, agencies, and new verticals such as med-spa and dermatology are the growth vectors management is betting on.** That explains the heavy developer and hackathon activity in 2026.

- I did not find the B2C revenue split for 2024.

## 3. Product lines and 2025–2026 launches

### Takeaway
Perfect Corp has two product sides:
- **Consumer apps:** a suite of YouCam apps that are increasingly GenAI photo and video tools rather than pure makeup try-on.
- **Enterprise:** makeup, hair, nail, jewelry, watch, eyewear, shoes, bags and clothes virtual try-on (VTO), AI Skin Analysis, foundation shade matching, face analysis, a "Beauty AI Agent," and the **YouCam API** (60+ REST features, prepaid units, and MCP servers for AI agents).

The 2025–2026 launches focus on:
- generative clothes try-on (May 2025);
- the Wannaby fashion acquisition (closed January 2025);
- nine new Fashion APIs with MCP support (January 2026);
- the AI Beauty Agent (CES 2026, Shoptalk 2026);
- agency project management for the API (June 2026);
- medical and dermatology use of Skin AI (Keensight, April 2026).

### Cited Findings
**Consumer apps (FY2025 20-F descriptions)**
- **YouCam Makeup:** "All-in-One AI beauty agent capable of real-time AR makeup applications, selfie retouch, hair color and style retouch, skin diagnosis…"
- **YouCam Perfect:** an AI photo editor and beauty camera, rated 4.8 on the App Store and 4.4 on Google Play.
- **YouCam AI Pro:** text-to-image and text-to-video.
- **YouCam Video:** a selfie video editor.
- **YouCam Enhance:** image-to-video, AI video repair, background tools and an AI avatar.
- **YouCam Nails**, **YouCam AI Chat**, and the **YouCam Online Editor** (web).
- Source: [FY2025 20-F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1899830/000189983026000013/perf-20251231.htm)
